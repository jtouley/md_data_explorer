Doctor: “Compare 30-day mortality by treatment in patients over 65.”
Assistant: “I’m identifying outcome, comparison group, and age filter.”

Read Results Like a Clinician

You do not need to become a statistician. Focus on three questions: Is this statistically robust? Is it clinically meaningful? Could bias explain it?

Statistical Signal

Look at uncertainty and confidence intervals, not just one p-value.

Clinical Meaning

Ask whether the effect size changes care decisions.

Caution

Association is not causation.

### Statistical vs. Clinical Significance

### Statistical Significance
- **Definition**: Unlikely to have occurred by chance (p<0.05)

### Clinical Significance
- **Definition**: Large enough difference to matter in practice
  1. A result can be mathematically “real” but too small to matter clinically.
  2. Large sample sizes can make trivial differences look impressive.
  3. Interpretation must combine statistics and bedside judgment.
Aha: “Significant” in software output and “important” in patient care are not the same word.
